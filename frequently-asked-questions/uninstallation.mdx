<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I archived an assessment. Why is the agent still installed?">
    Archiving sends an uninstall only to devices that are online at that moment. The confirmation says so:

    ![Archive confirmation explaining that only online devices are uninstalled](/images/uninstallation/uninstallation-1.png)

    Before archiving, check that the devices are online, or uninstall first with **Uninstall All**:

    ![Uninstall All button in the assessment's device list](/images/uninstallation/uninstallation-2.png)

    For devices that were offline, run the uninstall command on the device or through your RMM. See [Uninstall manually](/products/risk-assessments/uninstall-telivy-agent-windows#uninstall-manually).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to uninstall devices that were scanned without the agent?">
    No. Agentless scans leave nothing on the device. Those devices show an **Agentless Scan** badge and have no uninstall action.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
