  <Accordion title="What is the Telivy platform?">
    Telivy is a security assessment platform for MSPs. It scans a client's public attack
    surface, endpoints, and cloud accounts, scores the cyber risk it finds, recommends what
    to fix, and can keep monitoring the client on a schedule. These documents go into more
    detail:

    - [Telivy's one-page summary](https://storage.googleapis.com/cdn.telivy.com/marketing-assets/Telivy%201-Pager.pdf)
    - [Whitepaper: How to perform a cybersecurity risk assessment](https://storage.googleapis.com/cdn.telivy.com/marketing-assets/Whitepaper%20%231%20-%20How%20to%20Perform%20a%20Cybersecurity%20Risk%20Assessment%20v1.1.pdf)
    - [Whitepaper: Building trust and sales success with assessments](https://storage.googleapis.com/cdn.telivy.com/marketing-assets/Whitepaper%20%232%20-%20Building%20Trust%20and%20Sales%20Success%20v1.1.pdf)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I reset MFA?">
    If you've lost both your authenticator app and your recovery code, email
    [support@telivy.com](mailto:support@telivy.com) from the address registered with
    Telivy. Support may ask a few security questions about your account before resetting
    MFA.

    To register again afterwards, follow
    [Set Up Multi-Factor Authentication](/getting-started/set-up-telivy-account/multi-factor-authentication).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
