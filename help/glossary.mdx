Short definitions of the terms used in the Telivy portal and these docs, each with a link to
the page that covers it in full.

## Assessments and companies

### Company

A client or prospect in your account. The [Companies](/getting-started/companies) page lists
every company once, with its assessments grouped underneath.

### Prospect

A company you are trying to win rather than one you already manage. Prospects have their own
segment on the Companies page and can be monitored with **Monitor Prospect**.

### External Assessment (external scan)

A scan of a company's public-facing systems, such as domains, IP addresses, email security,
and SSL/TLS certificates, from the outside. It needs no agent and no access to the client's
network. See [External Assessments](/products/external-assessments/overview).

### Risk Assessment

Everything in an External Assessment plus data from the Telivy agent on the client's devices
and from their Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace tenant. See
[Risk Assessments](/products/risk-assessments/overview).

### Finding

One issue Telivy detected, such as a missing patch, an open port, or a breached password. The
**Risk** tab groups findings by issue. See [Risk and Priorities](/products/risk-and-priorities).

### Risk Register

The record of every decision to accept a finding or resolve it with a compensating control,
with the reason and a review date. See [Risk Register](/products/risk-register).

## Agent and scanning

### Telivy agent

Software installed on a Windows or Mac device that stays there, checks in once an hour, and
keeps scanning on a schedule. See [Agent vs Agentless](/products/risk-assessments/agent-vs-agentless).

### Agentless scan

A one-time scan run by the agentless scanner. It leaves nothing installed and gives a
snapshot, with no rescans or monitoring.

### Enhanced Agentless (Beta)

A separate Windows agentless scanner that runs with elevated system privileges, so it can
scan more than the standard agentless scanner, such as Chrome passwords. See
[Agent vs Agentless](/products/risk-assessments/agent-vs-agentless#enhanced-agentless-deployment-beta-on-windows).

### Remediator

A Windows component that applies the fixes you approve. It appears only when it is enabled
for your agency, with its status per endpoint under **Inventory > Endpoints**. See
[Inventory](/products/risk-assessments/inventory).

### Auto-update

Keeps the agent on each device current without reinstalling it. See
[Auto Update](/products/risk-assessments/auto-update).

## Scores and grades

### Risk Score

A number from 0 to 100 that summarizes a client's security posture in a Risk Assessment.
Higher is better. See [Risk Scoring](/scoring/risk-scoring).

### Risk Priority Score (RPS)

A 0 to 100 score given to every vulnerability (CVE) and every device with vulnerabilities, so
you know what to fix first. The **Priorities** tab ranks by it. See
[Risk Priority Score](/scoring/risk-scoring#risk-priority-score).

### Security grade

The A to F letter grade given to each section of the Security Report and the external scan.
See [Grading Criteria](/scoring/grading-criteria).

### CVSS and EPSS

CVSS rates how severe a vulnerability is. EPSS estimates how likely it is to be exploited in
the next 30 days. Both feed into the RPS. See
[Vulnerability data sources](/learning-center/vulnerability-data-sources).

### Insurability Score

A score on an External Assessment's Overview tab, based on the client's answers to the
assessment questionnaire.

### Financial Exposure

The card on every assessment's Overview tab that estimates what a cyber incident could cost
the client: ransomware, business interruption, and a data breach. See
[Data Security](/products/risk-assessments/data-security#financial-exposure-card).

### PII

Personally identifiable information, such as Social Security numbers, card numbers, and
health IDs, found in files on the client's devices. See
[Data Security](/products/risk-assessments/data-security).

## Monitoring and alerts

### Risk Monitoring (Continuous Monitoring)

Rescans a client on a schedule and saves a snapshot after each scan, so you can show how
their posture changes. See [Risk Monitoring](/products/risk-monitoring/overview).

### Alert

A notification that Telivy found something new between rescans, such as a new breach or a
new critical vulnerability. See [Alerts](/products/risk-monitoring/alerts).

### Alert policy

The settings that decide, for each alert category, which team members get an email and
whether matching findings open a ticket in your PSA. See
[Alerts](/products/risk-monitoring/alerts).

### PSA integration

The connection to ConnectWise, Autotask, or HaloPSA that opens tickets for findings and closes
the finding in Telivy when the ticket closes. See [Integrations](/integrations/overview#psa).

## Account

### Agency

Your MSP's account in Telivy. A **master agency** can have **sub-agencies** and work with
their assessments.

### Account Admin

A user who can manage the agency's users, billing, and integrations. See
[Account Setup](/getting-started/set-up-telivy-account/overview).

### Unity

Cytracom's partner platform. For agencies on Unity it handles signing in to Telivy,
subscriptions and billing, and the customer records Telivy clients are linked to. See
[Unity](/getting-started/set-up-telivy-account/unity).

### Early Access

Features that are switched on for specific agencies before general release. See
[Billing, Usage, and Early Access](/getting-started/set-up-telivy-account/billing-usage-and-early-access).

### Telivy MCP Server

Lets AI assistants such as Claude Code, Cursor, or GitHub Copilot in VS Code work with your
Telivy data in conversation. See
[Telivy MCP Server](/integrations/mcp/overview).
