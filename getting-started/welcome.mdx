Telivy, part of Cytracom, scans a company from the outside and the inside, scores what it
finds, and keeps scanning after the first report goes out. Agencies on Cytracom's Unity
platform should read [Unity and Cytracom](/getting-started/set-up-telivy-account/unity) for
sign-in and subscriptions.
