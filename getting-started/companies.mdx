## What the Companies page is for

**Companies** lists the organizations you work with, separately from the assessments run
against them. One company can have several assessments over time, and this page is the
single list that ties them together.

## The companies list

Each row is one client company. The columns are:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Status | Where the company stands with you, from early prospect to client |
| Company Name | The company and its domain |
| Industry | The industry recorded for the company |
| Category | Your own grouping for the company |
| Anti Virus | The antivirus product in use |
| PSA | Which PSA company it is mapped to, or **Not mapped**. Only shown when a PSA integration is enabled |
| Assessments | How many assessments belong to the company |
| Created | When the company was added |

Use **Search by company** to find a company by name, and the filter buttons to narrow the
list by **Status**, **Category**, or **Creation Date**.

## Managing a company

Open the menu at the end of a row to:

- **Edit** the company details, such as industry, category, and antivirus.
- **View Assessments** to jump to that company's assessments.
- **Map to PSA** (or **Change PSA mapping** if it is already mapped) to link the company to
  a company in ConnectWise, Autotask, or HaloPSA, so tickets for its findings land in the
  right place.
- **Delete** the company.

Filling in the category for every company is what makes the **Category** filter useful once
the list gets long.

## Deleting a company

Deleting a company also deletes every assessment that belongs to it, and it cannot be
undone. The confirmation dialog shows how many assessments will go with it.

Because of the amount of data involved, Telivy schedules the deletion and processes it in
the background. Until it finishes, the row shows **Company scheduled for deletion** and the
**Delete** action is disabled.

<Warning>
  If you only want to stop working on a client, archive their assessments instead. Archived
  assessments keep their history and can be unarchived later.
</Warning>
