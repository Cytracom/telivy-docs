## What the Companies page is for

**Companies** is the page Telivy opens after you log in. It lists every client company with
the assessments that belong to it, so one company with several assessments appears once,
with its assessments grouped underneath.

## Needs attention

The row at the top counts the companies that need work, by reason. Only reasons with a count
above zero are shown, and if nothing needs work the row reads **Nothing needs attention.**

| Chip | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| agents offline | Devices whose Telivy agent has stopped reporting |
| failed deployments | Devices where the agent failed to install |
| cloud connections | Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace connections that need fixing |
| new critical risks | Assessments with new critical findings |

Click a chip to filter the list to the companies behind it, and click it again (or
**Clear**) to remove the filter.

<Note>
  The agents offline and failed deployments counts are recalculated every 15 minutes and
  when a device's status changes, so they can lag behind the agent by a few minutes. The
  text **Device counts updated N minutes ago** at the right of the row shows when they were
  last recalculated. Hover over it to see the exact time.
</Note>

## Finding companies

The toolbar under the Needs attention row holds the controls for the list.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Views menu | Switches between preset views and the views you saved |
| **All**, **Clients**, **Prospects** | Limits the list to one segment, with a count for each |
| **Search companies or assessments** | Searches company names and assessment names |
| **Filters** | Narrows the list by company, assessment, or scope properties |
| **View** | Chooses how assessments are shown and which columns appear |

The preset views are:

| Preset | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Needs attention first | The default order, with companies that need work at the top |
| Prospecting | Prospects, newest first |
| Risk management | Clients, lowest score first |
| Monitoring | Only companies with monitoring turned on |
| Cloud setup | Companies where Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace is not fully connected |
| PSA cleanup | Companies not linked to your PSA. Only shown when a PSA integration is connected |

To keep your own combination of filters and columns, open the views menu and click **Save
current filters and columns**. **Make my default** opens that view whenever you return to the
page. Agency admins also see **Set as team default**, which makes the view the default for
everyone in the agency.

The **Filters** menu groups its filters under **Companies** (for example **Client status**,
**Category**, **Company created**, and **Has monitoring on**), **Assessments** (for example
**User**, **Status**, **Type**, **Source**, **Monitoring**, **Microsoft 365**, and **Google
Workspace**), and **Scope**. Which filters appear depends on your role and account. Active
filters are listed as chips under the toolbar, where you can remove them one at a time.

Under **View**, choose **Expanded** to list every assessment under its company, or **One row
per company** to collapse them. A company with a single assessment always shows it in its
row. The same menu turns these columns on and off: **Assessment Type**, **Status**,
**Score**, **Critical**, **Cloud**, **Monitoring**, and **Last scan**.

## Working with a company or an assessment

Open the menu at the end of an assessment row for:

- **Open assessment**
- **Rename**, or **Add a name** if the assessment has none
- **Turn on monitoring**, or **Change monitoring schedule** if it is already on
- **Download report**
- **Manage agents** on a risk assessment, or **Convert to risk assessment** on an external
  scan
- **Move to another company**
- **Archive assessment**

The same menu has a **Company** section with:

- **New assessment** for that company
- **Edit company**, to change its status, category, antivirus, and industries
- **Map to** your PSA, or **Change PSA mapping** if it is already linked. Only shown when a
  PSA integration is connected
- **Delete company**

## Bulk actions

Tick the boxes next to assessments to open the bulk action bar at the bottom of the page.

| Action | What it does | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reassign user** | Hands the selected assessments to another user, or to a user in a sub-agency | Admins |
| **New assessments** | Creates one new assessment for each company behind the selection | Anyone who can create assessments |
| **Archive** | Archives the selected assessments | Admins |

**New assessments** opens the same dialog used after a PSA import, described in
[creating assessments in bulk](#creating-assessments-in-bulk).

## Importing companies from your PSA

When ConnectWise, Autotask, or HaloPSA is connected, an **Import from** button for your PSA
appears in the toolbar. It shows how many PSA companies are new since you last looked. If no
PSA is connected, a notice at the top of the page links to the PSA settings instead.

The arrow next to the button opens a menu that shows how many of your PSA companies have an
assessment and how many are not linked to a Telivy company. It also contains:

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Import companies** | Opens the import. Reads **Import all N companies** before your first import |
| **Review N new in** your PSA | Opens the import with only the new PSA companies, already selected |
| **Dismiss N new** | Clears the new-company count without importing |
| **Link recommendations** | Lists Telivy companies that match exactly one PSA company, so you can link them in one step |
| **Check** your PSA **for changes** | Asks Telivy to sync the PSA company list now |
| **Sync settings** | Controls what Telivy does with new PSA companies on its own |

The import has three steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose companies">
    The list shows your PSA companies with their **Type**, **Status**, and, where your PSA
    has one, **Classification**. The **In Telivy** column says whether each company is
    **Not in Telivy**, **Matches** an existing Telivy company, **Needs review** because it
    matches more than one, or is **Already imported**. Search by name or domain, filter by
    any of the PSA's fields, and sort by any column. **Hide already imported** is on by
    default. The line above the table shows when the list was last synced, with a
    **Refresh** link.

    One import takes up to 500 companies. If more match, **Select first 500** selects a
    batch. Imported companies drop out of the list, so the next batch starts where the last
    one ended.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and import">
    Telivy shows what will happen to each company: new companies are created, and companies
    that already exist in Telivy are linked to their PSA record instead of duplicated. For a
    row that matches several Telivy companies, pick the right one under **Choose a company
    to link**. Tick **Mark new companies as clients** to give the created companies the
    Client status, then click **Import**.

    The result shows how many companies were created, linked, and skipped, with the reason
    for each skipped company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create assessments">
    Click **Continue to assessments** to create assessments for the imported companies, or
    **Skip for now** to do it later from the Companies list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If your PSA has a very large number of companies, the counts, filters, and sorting cover
  only the first part of the directory that Telivy has synced, and a note in the import
  says so. Search by name to find the others.
</Note>

### Sync settings

Telivy checks your PSA for new companies every few hours. **Sync settings** has three
switches, and each one depends on the one before it:

1. **Add new** companies **automatically** imports new PSA companies without you opening
   the import.
2. **Run an external scan for each new company** creates an external scan for each company
   added that way. If external scans are not in your plan, the dialog says so and none run.
3. **Send their findings to** your PSA **as tickets** turns on ticketing for those scans.

Assessments created by the sync are owned by the user who saved the settings, and the dialog
names that user.

## Creating assessments in bulk

The bulk creation dialog opens from **Continue to assessments** after a PSA import, or from
**New assessments** in the bulk action bar. To scan prospects that are not in Telivy yet, upload a CSV of domains instead. See [Bulk Create and Export External Scans](/products/external-assessments/create-and-export-in-bulk).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the type">
    Pick **Risk Assessment** or **External Assessment**. One run creates one type. A type
    your plan does not include is shown locked, with an upgrade or contact option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure">
    For risk assessments, choose a **Scan frequency** (**One-time scan**, weekly, monthly,
    or quarterly) if monitoring is in your plan. Each company gets its own risk assessment,
    and scanning starts once the agent is deployed on its devices.

    For external assessments, companies without a domain are listed with an **Add a domain**
    field. Fill it in to scan the company, or leave it blank to skip it. External results
    usually arrive within 10 minutes.

    If your PSA is connected and has an alert policy, tick **Send findings to** your PSA
    **as tickets** to ticket the results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the results">
    The dialog lists the assessments it created, each with a link to open it (or to set up
    the agent, for a risk assessment), and the companies it skipped.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Skip reason | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No domain to scan | Add a domain in the results and click **Retry with domains** |
| Already has an active assessment of this type | Nothing; the company is already covered |
| Not included in your plan | Upgrade the plan to create this type |
| Belongs to another account | The company is managed under another account |
| Could not be created, try again | Retry the creation |

<Note>
  If your master agency does not allow sub-agencies to create assessments, the bulk creation
  options are not shown.
</Note>

## Archiving assessments

There is no archive for a company itself. To stop working on a client, archive their
assessments, either with **Archive assessment** in the row menu or by selecting several and
clicking **Archive** in the bulk action bar.

An archived assessment drops out of the list but can still be found with a search or the
**Archived** option of the **Status** filter, and its reports stay available. Archiving a
risk assessment triggers an uninstall of the agent on every online device. Offline devices
keep the agent until someone runs the uninstall command on them.

The menu of an archived assessment offers **Unarchive** and **Delete assessment**. An
assessment has to be archived before it can be deleted.

## Deleting a company

**Delete company** removes the company and every assessment that belongs to it, and it
cannot be undone. The confirmation dialog states how many assessments will be deleted with
it. Telivy schedules the deletion and processes it in the background.

<Warning>
  If you only want to stop working on a client, archive their assessments instead. Archived
  assessments keep their history and can be unarchived later.
</Warning>
