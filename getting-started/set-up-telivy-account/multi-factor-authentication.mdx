A password on its own can leak through a breach, a phishing page, or reuse on another
site. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) means a stolen password isn't enough: whoever logs
in also needs a one-time code from your phone. Your Telivy account holds client data and
payment details, so MFA is on by default for every account.

## Registering for MFA

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    The first time you log in, Telivy asks you to register an authenticator app such as
    Google Authenticator. Scan the QR code with the app.

    If the app can't scan it, click **Trouble Scanning?** to show the seed phrase and type
    that into the app instead.

    <img alt="MFA registration screen with a QR code and the seed phrase option" src="/images/multi-factor-authentication/register-mfa-1.jpg" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the one-time code">
    Type the one-time code the authenticator app now shows.

    <img alt="One-time code entry screen during MFA registration" src="/images/multi-factor-authentication/register-mfa-2.jpg" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your recovery code">
    Click **Continue**. The next screen shows a recovery code. It lets you log in if you
    lose your phone or the authenticator app, so store it somewhere safe, such as a password
    manager.

    <img alt="Recovery code screen shown after MFA registration" src="/images/multi-factor-authentication/register-mfa-3.jpg" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm you saved it">
    Tick **I have safely recorded this code**. Registration is complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Logging in with MFA

When you log in to the Telivy portal, you may be asked for an MFA code.

<img alt="MFA code prompt on the Telivy portal login screen" src="/images/multi-factor-authentication/login-mfa.jpg" />

Enter the current code from your authenticator app. On a device you trust, you can choose
to remember it for 30 days, and Telivy won't ask for a code on that device until then.

If you don't have the authenticator app with you, choose **Try another method** and enter
your recovery code.

## Lost your device and recovery code?

Email [support@telivy.com](mailto:support@telivy.com) from the address registered with
Telivy. Support may need to verify your identity before resetting MFA on the account.
