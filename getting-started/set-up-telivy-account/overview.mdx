The **Account** area holds your agency's Telivy settings, and only account admins can open
it. The sections below follow the order of the side menu.

| Section | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Profile | Your details, the agency name and logo, and a usage snapshot |
| Users | Inviting teammates and setting what they can see |
| Assessment Defaults | Default PII capture settings and the agent update channel for new assessments |
| Lead Magnet | Your lead magnet link, for Lead Magnet subscribers |
| Integrations | PSA, Nodeware, and other connections |
| Unity | Unity-linked agencies only. See [Billing, Usage, and Early Access](/getting-started/set-up-telivy-account/billing-usage-and-early-access#unity) |
| Webhooks | Sending Telivy events to your own systems. See [Webhooks](/integrations/webhooks) |
| Early Access, Billing, Usage | See [Billing, Usage, and Early Access](/getting-started/set-up-telivy-account/billing-usage-and-early-access) |

## Users: managing your team

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Permission levels">
    The **Users** page lists everyone in your organization with their permission level.
    There are two:

    - **All Assessments**: the user can open every assessment in your organization. This
      makes them an Account Admin.
    - **Own Assessments**: the user can open assessments they created, plus any that an
      admin or the owner has shared with them. See "Sharing an assessment with a teammate"
      below.

    ![Team Members list with each user's role, risk assessments and assets](/images/access-control-made-simple.jpg)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Adding a user">
    Click **Add User**, enter their details, and choose a permission level.

    ![Add User dialog with name, email, password and the Own Assessments or All Assessments role](/images/expand-your-team.png)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sharing an assessment with a teammate">
    To let a teammate help on one assessment without giving them access to every
    assessment, open the assessment, click **More**, and choose **Manage Access**.

    These people can manage access to an assessment:
    - The assessment **owner** (whoever created it, or whoever it was reassigned to)
    - **Account Admins** in the same account
    - **Telivy Admins**

    A shared user has the same view and edit rights as the owner on that one assessment.
    They can rescan, view reports, archive, convert, and receive the same hourly alert
    emails. They can't add or remove other shared users, reassign the assessment, or see any
    assessment they weren't added to.

    Removing a shared user takes away their access immediately. The assessment keeps an
    audit record of who was shared, who removed them, and when.

    If you use Unity, your Unity role (Telivy User or Telivy Admin) still decides whether
    you are an Account Admin in Telivy. Sharing one assessment with a Telivy User doesn't
    change their Unity role or give them access to anything else.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reassigning and archiving assessments">
    Reassigning and sharing do different things:

    - **Reassign** hands full ownership of an assessment to another user. The previous
      owner loses access unless they were also added as a shared user. Use it when someone
      leaves or a client moves to a different account manager for good.
    - **Share** adds a teammate without removing anyone. Use it for short-term help or
      cover.

    To reassign, tick the assessments on the Assessments list and click **Reassign**. The
    button shows how many you selected. In **Confirm reassigning assessments user**, choose
    the new owner under **Assign assessments to** and click **Confirm**. Only admins can
    reassign. The **Archive** button next to it archives the selected assessments instead.

    ![Bulk reassign or archive assessments from the dashboard](/images/reassigning-archiving-assessments.png)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reassigning assessments to a sub-agency">
    If your agency is a master agency with sub-agencies, the reassign dialog asks for an
    **Agency** first and then lists the users in that agency. Your own agency is at the top
    and sub-agencies follow in alphabetical order. With eight or more sub-agencies, a
    **Search agencies** box appears at the top of the list.

    When an assessment moves to a user in another agency, Telivy files it under a matching
    company in that agency and creates the company there if needed. Account Admins can
    reassign to users in their own agency and its sub-agencies.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Assessment Defaults

**Assessment Defaults** sets what new assessments start with:

- **PII Capture**: the default sensitive-data settings for new Risk Assessments. See
  [Data Security](/products/risk-assessments/data-security).
- **Agent Updates**: which release channel the Telivy agent follows. See
  [Auto Update](/products/risk-assessments/auto-update).
