Telivy is part of Cytracom. Unity is Cytracom's partner platform, and for agencies on Unity it
handles three things for Telivy: signing in, subscriptions and billing, and the customer
records that Telivy clients are linked to. Agencies that are not on Unity sign in with a Telivy
email and password and are billed through the Stripe portal described in
[Billing, Usage, and Early Access](/getting-started/set-up-telivy-account/billing-usage-and-early-access).

## Signing in through Unity

On the Telivy login page, click **Sign in with Cytracom** to sign in with your Unity account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept the terms">
    The first time you sign in through Unity, Telivy shows **Terms and Conditions**. Tick
    **I have read and agree to Telivy's Terms & Conditions** and click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Merge an existing account, if Telivy finds one">
    If a Telivy account with the same email address already exists, Telivy shows
    **Existing Account Found** with that account's email, creation date, and agency. Click
    **Merge Accounts** to keep the data from the existing account, or **Create New Account**
    to start a separate account without it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your Unity account has no Telivy access, Telivy shows **Telivy Access Required** instead
of signing you in. Click **Go to Unity Marketplace** and subscribe to Telivy there, then sign
in again.

When your agency is on Unity, users are invited and managed in Unity. The **Users** page in
Telivy shows a **Manage Users** button that opens Unity's settings, and your agency name and
logo come from Unity.

### When your agency has moved to Unity

After an agency moves to Unity, its users can no longer use their old Telivy email and
password. A user who signs in that way sees **Account migration required**, which tells them
to ask their administrator for a Unity invitation. Once they accept the invitation and sign in
to Telivy through Unity, all of their previous data is available. The screen offers
**Go to Unity** and **Log out**, and it does not let the user continue into Telivy.

### Merge Unity Accounts

**Account > Merge Unity Accounts** lists duplicate users and agencies, for example when
someone created a new account during their first Unity sign-in instead of merging. Merging
moves all assets and data to the primary account. Only Telivy staff can see this page, so
contact [support@telivy.com](mailto:support@telivy.com) if you end up with two accounts.

## Subscriptions and what they unlock

| Subscription | Scope | What it unlocks in Telivy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assess | Your whole agency | Unlimited External Assessments and Risk Assessments for any company |
| Monitor Core | One client | Continuous monitoring, recurring scans, and risk-change alerts |
| Monitor Pro | One client | Everything in Monitor Core, plus remediation workflows and compliance |
| Cytracom Foundations | One client, added in Unity | Telivy coverage bundled with other Cytracom products |

Cytracom Foundations has three tiers in Unity: Core, Advanced, and Premium. Foundations Core
does not include Telivy. A client on Foundations Advanced or Foundations Premium gets Telivy
monitoring as part of the bundle and can have a Risk Assessment even without Assess, and
remediation workflows come with Foundations Premium. Agencies on Foundations add and change
coverage in Unity, so Telivy never offers them an in-app purchase.

The **Monitor Foundation** badge on the Unity tab is an older Monitor plan. It is a different
product from Cytracom Foundations.

### What the Create Assessment cards say

When you click **Create Assessment**, each card states whether your plan covers it. Unity adds
these variations:

| Card shows | Meaning | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not included in your plan, Available with Assess, billed through Unity | Your agency needs Assess for this assessment type | Admins click **Unlock with Assess**. Other users see "Ask your agency admin to enable Assess to unlock this." **View pricing on Unity** opens the price in Unity |
| Not included in your plan, Available through Unity | Your agency is on Foundations and adds this in Unity | Admins click **Manage in Unity**. Other users see "Ask your agency admin to add this in Unity." |
| Included with Foundations coverage | The Monitored Risk Assessment card, for a Foundations agency | Coverage is added per company in Unity |
| Not included in your plan, Billed per client. You choose the plan next | The Monitored Risk Assessment card, for an agency that buys Monitor | You pick Monitor Core or Monitor Pro after choosing the client |

The **Monitored Risk Assessment** card appears only for agency admins who signed in through
Unity. See [Start a Risk Assessment](/products/risk-assessments/start-a-new-risk-assessment)
for the cards that every agency sees.

### Buying in the app

Agency admins on Unity can buy and upgrade subscriptions in Telivy. Every purchase is a
recurring charge billed to your Unity account, and any coupons apply in Unity.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Assess">
    Click **Unlock with Assess** on a locked card, or **Purchase Assess** on the Unity tab.
    The **Subscribe to Assess** screen explains that one subscription covers the whole agency.
    Click **Confirm & subscribe**. The External Assessment and Risk Assessment cards unlock
    as soon as Telivy shows "Assess enabled for your agency".
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Monitor">
    Choose the **Monitored Risk Assessment** card. The wizard has three steps: **Client**,
    where you pick or add the client and its Unity customer; **Plan**, where you choose
    **Monitor Core** or **Monitor Pro**; and **Schedule**, where you choose a weekly, monthly,
    or quarterly snapshot. Scans run daily, and the schedule only sets how often Telivy
    captures a snapshot.

    For a client that already has an assessment, use **Add Monitoring** or
    **Upgrade to Pro** on the Unity tab.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Foundations">
    The Monitored Risk Assessment wizard has only **Client** and **Schedule**. A client
    appears in the picker once Foundations coverage has been added to it in Unity and has
    synced to Telivy. **Open Unity Foundations** takes you to Unity to add it.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

A feature that the plan doesn't include shows the action that unlocks it, such as
**Subscribe to Assess**, **Link to Unity**, **Add monitoring**, or
**Upgrade to Monitor Pro**. Users who are not admins are asked to contact their agency admin.

## Linking Telivy clients to Unity customers

Monitoring, Monitor plans, and the [Tentacle integration](/integrations/tentacle) all need the
Telivy client to be linked to a Unity customer. Monitoring is only available for clients, so a
prospect has to be converted to a client before it can be monitored.

### The Unity Sync modal

When you sign in, Telivy checks Unity for customers that have an active Telivy subscription
but aren't linked to a Telivy client yet.

- A customer that has a domain and no likely match among your Telivy clients is added as a new
  Telivy client automatically. Telivy then shows **Your Unity clients are now in Telivy**,
  from which you can open or start each client's Risk Assessment.
- Any other customer is listed in **Connect your Unity clients to Telivy**. For each one, the
  **Link to** menu offers your existing Telivy clients or **Create a new Telivy client**. A
  client with the same domain is marked **Exact match** and preselected. A similar client is
  marked **Best match** but is not selected for you. A new client needs a domain if the Unity
  record doesn't have one.

Click **Sync clients** to apply your choices. **Not now** hides the listed customers for the
rest of your session, and a customer that appears in Unity later still opens the modal.
**Manage all Unity syncs** goes to the Unity tab.

### The sync banner

While Unity customers are still waiting to be linked, a banner at the top of the app reads
"N Unity clients ready to sync to Telivy". **Open sync** reopens the modal with every
waiting customer, including the ones you hid. The **X** hides the banner for 24 hours.

### The Unity tab

**Account > Integrations > Unity** lists every company with its Unity link and
subscriptions. It appears only for users who signed in through Unity.

The top of the page shows whether Assess is active for the agency, with **Purchase Assess**
for admins when it isn't. If Unity customers are waiting to be connected, a banner offers
**Review & connect**, which opens the Unity Sync modal.

The table can be filtered by **All**, **Linked**, and **Not in Unity**, and searched by name
or domain. The **Unity** column shows one of three states:

| Unity column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Linked | The client is linked to a Unity customer. Hover to see which one |
| Link | A Unity customer matches this client's domain. Click to link them |
| Not in Unity | No Unity customer matches. **Add to Unity** creates a Unity customer or links an existing one |

**Bulk add to Unity** creates a Unity customer for every client that isn't connected yet,
using the address on the client's assessment. Prospects are never included. A client without
a usable address is marked **Needs address** or **Needs attention**, and you can add one with
**Add billing address**. Click **Create N Unity customers** to start. The progress view lets you
retry failed rows, and you can resume the run if your Unity session expires.

The **Action** column shows the next useful step for each client:

- **View** or **Open** opens the client's Risk Assessment, or creates one. Creating one needs
  Assess, a Monitor subscription on that client, or Foundations coverage for that client.
- **New assessment** starts a fresh Risk Assessment when every assessment for the client is
  archived. **View** still opens the archived one.
- **Enable Monitoring** starts recurring scans for a client whose Monitor or Foundations
  coverage is active but not yet scanning.
- **Add Monitoring** and **Upgrade to Pro** buy a Monitor plan or move a client from Monitor
  Core to Monitor Pro. Only admins see them.
- **Manage in Unity** and **Add coverage in Unity** open Unity for Foundations clients and
  for agencies on Foundations.

### Linking from an assessment

The assessment's integrations configuration has a **Unity** tile marked **Connected** or
**Not Connected**. Click a tile that isn't connected to open **Link Unity Client**, then choose
**Create New** to create a Unity client or **Link Existing** to search for one. A connected tile
shows the subscriptions on the linked customer and **Manage Unity sync**, which opens the
Unity tab.
