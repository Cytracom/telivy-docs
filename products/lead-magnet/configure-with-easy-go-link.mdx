The Easy-Go link is the quickest way to put the Lead Magnet in front of prospects. It opens a Telivy-hosted form where a prospect enters their business details and gets a security assessment, and every submission lands in your Telivy account. There is no code to write.

## Find your link

In Telivy, go to **Account → Lead Magnet**. The **Easy-Go Link** card shows **Your form link**, which looks like this:

```
https://portal.telivy.com/start/{your-agent-id}
```

## Put the link where prospects will see it

- **On your website:** add a button in your website builder (Wix, WordPress, Webflow or similar), styled to match the rest of the site, and point it at the link.
- **In emails, newsletters or flyers:** use the link as is, or turn it into a QR code for print.

To see which campaign each submission came from, add a UTM source to the link as described in [Track submissions with UTM source](/products/lead-magnet/track-submissions-with-utm-source).
