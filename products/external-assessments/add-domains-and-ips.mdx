An assessment starts with the one domain you entered when you created it. Most companies
are exposed in more places than that: a marketing site on another domain, a VPN endpoint, a
mail gateway, a block of static IPs. Add them as targets and they get scanned alongside the
original domain.

## Add a target

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the domains list">
    In an External Scan, go to **Setup > Manage Domains**. In a Risk Assessment, use
    **Inventory > Manage Domains and IP Addresses**. The table lists every target with its
    type, scanning status, when it was last scanned, and how many findings it has.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Target">
    Choose the target type: **IP Address**, **Domain**, or **Subdomain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the values">
    Type the value, or paste several separated by commas. Telivy checks the format first and
    rejects a malformed address or domain instead of saving it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add Target**. The new targets appear in the table and are picked up by the next
    scan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Adding a target that is already there changes nothing. The portal says it's already added
  and no duplicate appears.
</Note>

If **Add Target** isn't available, your account needs an upgrade to scan extra targets.
Email [accounts@telivy.com](mailto:accounts@telivy.com).

## What to add

- Every domain the company owns, including ones that only redirect
- Subdomains that resolve to something reachable, such as `vpn.`, `mail.`, `remote.`, or `portal.`
- Static IP addresses for on-premises firewalls, VPN concentrators, and mail servers

Each target gets the same checks as the primary domain, so open ports and certificate
problems on a forgotten subdomain show up in
[the same findings](/products/external-assessments/overview).
