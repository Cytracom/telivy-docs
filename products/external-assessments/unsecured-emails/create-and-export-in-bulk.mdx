## Create external scans from a CSV

Use the bulk upload to scan a list of prospects in one go.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the bulk upload">
    Click **Create Assessment**, select the **External Scan** card, and click
    **Upload Multiple Domains**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the CSV">
    Click **Download Sample CSV** for the template. It has two columns, `domain` and
    `companyName`, with one company per row. You can submit up to 50 domains per file,
    unless your account has a different limit, which the dialog shows.

    <img alt="Download Sample CSV option in the bulk upload dialog" src="/images/create-and-export-in-bulk/create-and-export-in-bulk-1.png" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the file">
    Choose the file under **Upload CSV File**. The scans appear in your portal and start
    straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Export findings from all external scans

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the export">
    Click **Export External Scans (CSV)** at the bottom of the Assessments list.

    <img alt="Export External Scans (CSV) link at the bottom of the dashboard" src="/images/create-and-export-in-bulk/create-and-export-in-bulk-2.png" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a date range (optional)">
    Set a **Start Date** and **End Date** to export only scans from that period. Leave them
    empty to export every external scan in your account.

    <img alt="Date range selection dialog for the external scans CSV export" src="/images/create-and-export-in-bulk/create-and-export-in-bulk-3.png" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    Click **Download**. The CSV has the grade for each section and the top 10 findings from
    each section, along with details such as the last scan time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
