## Overview

Rescans show how a client's posture moved over a period. Alerts tell you about a specific change as soon as Telivy finds it: a new dark web breach, a look-alike domain, a new critical vulnerability on an endpoint, or a Microsoft 365 account without MFA.

Alert policies decide two things for each category: which of your team members get an email, and whether matching findings open a ticket in your connected PSA. Policies are set once for the whole agency and apply to every assessment.

## The Alerts page

Open **Alerts** in the Telivy portal to see every alert raised across your clients, newest first. Each row shows:

| Column | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Detected** | When Telivy raised the alert. Unread alerts are marked. |
| **Alert** | What was detected and which category it belongs to. |
| **Risk** | How urgent it is. Telivy uses the finding's [Risk Priority Score](/scoring/risk-scoring) where one exists, then the finding's severity, then the severity set on the policy. Hover the value to see which one it is. Activity records, such as a sign-in event, are labelled separately from standing risks. |
| **Affected** | The device, account, mailbox or domain the alert is about, and the state of the finding behind it: **Open**, **Accepted**, **Resolved**, **Ticketed**, or **No open item**. |
| **Client** | The assessment the alert was raised on. Click it to open that assessment. |

Click an alert that belongs to a finding and Telivy opens the assessment's **Risk** tab filtered to that finding, so you can accept it, resolve it, or open a PSA ticket from there. Other alerts open their own details page.

Use the filters to show unread alerts, a single category, or a time window such as **In the last 7 days**. **Mark N as read** clears the unread markers, and the chart button shows **Alerts over time**. Each assessment also has its own **Alerts** tab with the same list for that client.

## Alert email

Alerts are emailed once an hour as a digest, so a busy hour produces one email instead of one per finding. Only users selected on a policy receive it.

The digest groups alerts by client. For each alert it shows the risk level, what was detected, the affected device or account, the evidence behind it, a short recommended action, and a link to investigate in Telivy. Activity records, such as sign-in events, follow under their own heading so they are not mistaken for open risks. When a client has more alerts than fit, the email says how many more are waiting in the portal.

## Alert policies

Go to **Alerts → Policies**. The page lists every alert category in one table, grouped by source (for example Dark Web, TypoSquatting, Internal Vulnerabilities, Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace), with a count of how many are enabled.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Source** | The area the category belongs to. |
| **When** | The category and the condition that fires it. Categories Telivy recommends carry a **Recommended** badge. |
| **Enabled** | A switch to turn the policy on or off. |
| **Users to Notify** | How many team members receive the email for this policy. |
| **Delivery** | Whether the policy also opens PSA tickets, and from what severity. |

Search by name, or filter by source, by enabled status, or by PSA delivery.

### Turn on the recommended set

Click **Enable recommended** to add policies for every recommended category that does not have one yet. The **Apply recommended policies** dialog shows how many categories and users it will add. Existing policies are left untouched, so you can run it again safely after Telivy adds a category.

### Add or edit a policy

1. Click **Add Policy**, or **Edit** on an existing row.
2. In **Configure Alert Policy**, choose the **Alert Category** and the **Trigger Condition**. Some categories take an **Alert Metric** and a **Value**, for example a CVSS or EPSS threshold for Internal Vulnerabilities.
3. Click **Next: Select Users**.
4. In **Configure Alert Delivery**, select the users to email. If a PSA is connected, the **Open a PSA ticket** switch also sends this category's findings to it.
5. Click **Save**.

A policy needs at least one recipient or PSA delivery. Saving with neither removes the policy.

### Change many policies at once

Tick rows to select them, then use the bar above the table:

- **Enable** or **Disable** the selected policies. Disabling keeps the policies in the list, but their recipients are not kept.
- **Set recipients…** makes every selected policy email exactly the users you pick.
- **Turn off email** removes every recipient from the selected policies without touching PSA ticket sync.
- **Enable PSA sync** or **Disable PSA sync** for the selected categories.

## Send findings to your PSA

With [ConnectWise](/integrations/connectwise), [Autotask](/integrations/autotask) or [HaloPSA](/integrations/halopsa) connected, alert policies also decide which findings open PSA tickets. Turn on PSA sync for a category from **Edit** (the **Open a PSA ticket** switch) or with **Enable PSA sync** in bulk. The **Delivery** column then shows **PSA** with the severity floor, such as **High and above**.

PSA sync covers every finding category in the list, including external-surface ones such as Email / DNS Authentication or Web Application Security that do not send alert emails yet. A ticket is only opened when the client's company is mapped to the PSA and **Sync findings to PSA** is on for its assessment.

## Alert categories

Every category appears in **Alerts → Policies**. These categories raise alerts today:

| Category | What triggers it |
|---|---|
| Internal Vulnerabilities | New CVEs found on managed endpoints during a scan, optionally above a severity, CVSS or EPSS threshold |
| TypoSquatting Domains | A newly registered look-alike domain that impersonates the client |
| Dark Web Breach | A client domain or asset appears in a newly indexed data breach |
| Dark Web Account | A specific user's credentials appear in a breach dataset |
| Microsoft 365 Posture | A new Microsoft 365 posture finding, raised from an actionable Microsoft Secure Score control on a connected tenant |
| Microsoft 365 Failed Logins | Repeated failed sign-ins on a Microsoft 365 account |
| Microsoft 365 No MFA User | A Microsoft 365 account with no MFA method registered |
| Google Workspace Failed Logins | Repeated failed sign-ins on a Google Workspace account |
| Google Workspace No MFA User | A Google Workspace account without MFA |

### Rolling out

The other Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace categories in the list are being rolled out and do not raise alerts yet. They include Authentication Token Revoked, MFA Failed, MFA Disabled, MFA Enabled, Password Reset, Login Using Token, Login From Unapproved Location, User Create, User Delete, Registered Device, Admin Role Assignment, Group Membership Change, and Admin Policy Change, plus Conditional Access Violation for Microsoft 365 only. Microsoft 365 mailbox and file activity categories, such as Mailbox Forwarding Rule Created, External File Sharing and Mass File Deletion, are also in the list ahead of their rollout. You can create policies for these categories now, and they start firing once the category goes live.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How quickly does an alert fire after an event is detected?">
    Cloud events (Microsoft 365, Google Workspace) are evaluated on each sync. Endpoint alerts such as Internal Vulnerabilities are evaluated after each completed agent scan, and a manual rescan triggers evaluation straight away. The alert shows on the Alerts page as soon as it is raised. The email follows in the next hourly digest. See [Scanning Schedule](/products/risk-monitoring/scanning-schedule) for when each kind of data is collected.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I notify several team members from one policy?">
    Yes. Select as many agency users as you need in **Configure Alert Delivery**, or use **Set recipients…** to set them on several policies at once.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are alert policies scoped to individual clients?">
    No. Policies are set at the agency level and apply to every assessment. Each assessment's **Alerts** tab shows the alerts raised for that client.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do alerts require Risk Monitoring to be enabled?">
    No. Alerts are raised for any eligible Risk Assessment whenever new data arrives. Risk Monitoring adds the recurring rescans that keep that data fresh.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I stop the emails but keep PSA tickets?">
    Yes. Select the policies and use **Turn off email**. PSA ticket sync on those categories keeps working.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I send alerts to another system?">
    Yes. Subscribe to the `ALERT_RAISED` event with a [webhook](/integrations/webhooks) to receive each alert as an HTTP request.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
