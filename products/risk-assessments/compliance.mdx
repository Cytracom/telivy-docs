The **Compliance** tab on a Risk Assessment tracks the client against the CIS Critical Security Controls (153 safeguards). There is no questionnaire to fill in: Telivy maps what the scan found onto the CIS safeguards, so each answer comes from endpoint and cloud evidence.

## What the tab shows

The tab lists the CIS controls, each with a compliance status and severity, and shows the client's overall CIS posture. Use the category filter to look at one CIS control group. A category's status is worked out from the controls inside it.

## Requirements

Compliance is available on any Risk Assessment. Live CIS data comes through the **Tentacle** integration, which needs a linked Unity customer with an active Tentacle subscription. Until that is set up, the tab shows a setup prompt instead of controls. The [Tentacle guide](/integrations/tentacle) has the setup steps.
