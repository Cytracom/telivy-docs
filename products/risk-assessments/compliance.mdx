The **Compliance** tab on a Risk Assessment tracks the client against the **CIS Critical Security Controls** (153 safeguards). Rather than starting from a blank questionnaire, Telivy maps what the scan already found onto the CIS safeguards, so the answers come from real endpoint and cloud evidence.

## What the tab shows

The **Compliance** tab lists the CIS controls with a compliance status and severity for each one, a category filter to focus on one CIS control group, and the client's overall CIS posture. A category's status is worked out from the controls inside it.

## Requirements

Compliance is available on any Risk Assessment. Live CIS data comes through the **Tentacle** integration, which needs a linked Unity customer with an active Tentacle subscription. Until that is set up, the tab shows a setup prompt instead of controls. The [Tentacle guide](/integrations/tentacle) has the setup steps.
